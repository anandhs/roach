🖥️ Wait—What is a Server?

Think of a server like a giant, super-powered gaming PC sitting in a warehouse (called a data center) with no monitor or keyboard. When you tap "Play" on your phone or Chromebook, your device talks to a server over the internet to load the map, track your score, and connect you with your friends!
